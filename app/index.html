    Roadmap to platinum

    How to read this guide

    Every trophy shows its official name, description and grade exactly as PlayStation publishes them, plus a guide in our own words and the sources beside it.

    A guide that says a step “has not been checked in the game” means exactly that: the official description does not name it, and no sourced guide we cite confirms it. We would rather say so than invent a location or a mission name.

    No online trophies are listed: the official list of 55 holds none under the online kind, so the Online filter is empty by design.

    Missable trophies

    Why these are flagged

    A trophy is flagged missable when its official description describes a single clean attempt: one robbery without being seen, one escape, one use of Combat Focus, one set of choices. Story chapter and mission trophies are not flagged — they pop by playing the story.

    Rule of thumb: do the missables before the final cleanup, and save first where the game allows it.
