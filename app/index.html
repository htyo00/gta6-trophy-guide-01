How to read this roadmap

Work the stages in order, but do the side content as it appears — most questlines and activities open during the story and are easier to finish on the spot than to hunt down later. Every stage shows how many of its trophies you have earned, which trophies it holds, and which sources its steps cite.

Missable watch list

Only one trophy is possibly missable: Positive Mental Attitude — do its Chapter 1 balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends. Two more are disputed (#25 Strangers & Freaks, #37 Catch Me If You Can) and six story trophies are on watch for unverified one-time-window claims. The full list, with what the sources confirm and what they do not, is on the Missables tab.

Ground rules

  • No mission replay and no New Game+. There are 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave — keep a save at the start and end of each chapter (10 saves), and one before the final mission.
  • Cheats disable trophies and saving on that save. Keep your trophy run cheat-free.
  • No difficulty setting, no mission medals, no online trophies. One playthrough is enough.
  • Free roam continues after the story, and everything on the 100% checklist stays available.
  • The Irredeemable criminal profile is permanent — avoid it. Jason and Lucia's relationship status does not affect any trophy.

How this roadmap was built

Seven stages, ordered by chapter and then by grind. The stage order follows the guides' own three-step roadmap (story 60–80 hours, 100% checklist about 100 hours, Strangers & Freaks cleanup about 10 hours), split by chapter so the missable step lands where it belongs: Stage 1.

Nine trophies need a second look. One is possibly missable, two are disputed between the guides, and six carry a one-time-window claim that no guide read here could verify. Everything else — 46 trophies — is safe after the credits.
