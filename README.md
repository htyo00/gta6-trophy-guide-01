An offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of
Grand Theft Auto VI, built for the make.fun bounty
[gta6-trophy-guide-01](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01).

## What is inside

- All 55 trophies with their official names, descriptions and grades:
  1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze; 28 hidden on PlayStation.
- A guide for every trophy in our own words: 162 numbered steps,
  every step citing its source, from 7 sources read 20 Nov 2026
  (PowerPyx, OutputLag, KeenGamer, eGamers, Exophase, GTABase,
  and the official list shipped in the competition kit).
- A 7-stage roadmap to the platinum, and honest missable flags:
  1 possibly missable, 2 disputed, 6 watch, the rest safe.
  Claims the sources do not confirm are labelled unconfirmed,
  and disagreements between sources are shown with both versions.
- A tracker: a checklist saved in the browser (localStorage),
  export and import as JSON, filters by kind, and a spoiler
  setting that hides the 28 hidden trophies until earned.

## Run it

Serve `app/` with any static HTTP server and open `index.html`.
It works with the network off. `main.ts` copies `app/` and
`screenshots/` to `/output` and writes `/output/dossier.json`.
